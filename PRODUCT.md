# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Overview
A personal portfolio website for Dang Duc Tuan, a DevOps & Backend Engineering Intern. The platform serves as a digital resume and professional showcase to highlight skills, hands-on projects, and career aspirations in backend systems and cloud infrastructure.

## 2. Target Audience
- **Primary Audience:** Tech Recruiters and Talent Acquisition Specialists looking for internship or entry-level candidates.
- **Secondary Audience:** Engineering Managers and Senior Developers (Backend/DevOps) evaluating the candidate's technical depth and project experience.
- **Tertiary Audience:** Peers, classmates, and the broader tech community for networking purposes.

## 3. Brand Identity
- **Tone:** Professional, proactive, clear, and modern.
- **Vibe:** Minimalist, clean, and highly readable. It avoids unnecessary clutter, directing the user's attention straight to the candidate's value proposition.
- **Core Message:** A passionate, hands-on second-year IT student who is actively building systems and ready to contribute to real-world CI/CD, containerization, and backend projects.
